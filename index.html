Summary

I catch what breaks in Dynamics 365 F&O before your customers do — leading QA teams through testing, defect tracking, and releases across retail, supply chain, and finance systems.

I've tested systems across seven industries — retail, manufacturing, healthcare, fashion, stock exchange, financial services, and reinsurance. That range means I catch issues that QA engineers who only know one industry often miss.

Technically, I run performance, functional, regression, smoke, integration, and API testing (including GraphQL and payload validation), using tools like Postman, Selenium, JMeter, JIRA, and Azure DevOps. Across my last three roles alone, that approach has cut defect leakage by 30%+, improved regression efficiency by 35%, and supported zero-critical-defect go-lives.
